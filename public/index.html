📖 Rules of the Game

🎯 How to Play

  1. Register — Enter your name, Amazon alias, pick the country you support, and select your work team.
  2. Predict — For each World Cup match, enter your predicted score (e.g. 2-1).
  3. Score points — Earn points based on how accurate your predictions are.
  4. Climb the leaderboard — Compete individually and as a team!

⚽ Scoring System

⏰ Deadlines

  • Predictions lock at midnight (00:00) on the day of the match.
  • You can update predictions as many times as you want before the deadline.

🏆 Leaderboard

  • Individual — Ranked by total points. Tiebreaker: most exact scores.
  • Team — Ranked by average points per member (so team size doesn't matter).
  • 🔥 Streaks — Longest run of consecutive correct predictions (outcome or exact score).
  • 🎯 Expert Picks — Longest run of consecutive exact score predictions (3 pts each).
